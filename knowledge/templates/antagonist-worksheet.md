# Template: Antagonist Worksheet

The antagonist is built to attack the protagonist's exact weakness. Copy into the project's characters folder.

- Name (or nature, if not a person):
- What they want: incompatible with the protagonist's want, so one wins only if the other loses.
- Their logic: why they do what they do, from the inside.
- What makes them sympathetic: the comprehensible, even human reason a reader can understand.
- The protagonist's specific weakness: the wound or false belief.
- How they attack it: the exact way this antagonist exploits that weakness.
- How they escalate: how the pressure they apply rises across the book. They must be active, never passive.

If the antagonist is a system, place, illness, time, or the character's own nature, it still needs an active, escalating force that presses on the wound.
