# Template: Beat Sheet

Map the whole novel. Three-act with target positions. Fill each with what happens in your book. Percentages are guides, not laws. Copy into the project's beats.md.

Act One (~25%):
- Opening/Hook (0%): voice, character, normal life with tension present.
- Inciting Incident (10-15%): the event that breaks normal life.
- Point of No Return (20-25%): commits to the conflict, can't go back.

Act Two (~50%):
- Rising Obstacles (25-50%): try, fail, try harder. Each setback costs more.
- Midpoint (50%): reversal or revelation. Stakes rise. Shifts from reacting to acting.
- Things Tighten (50-75%): opposition presses, false victories collapse.
- All Is Lost (75%): lowest point. Goal looks impossible, false belief exposed.

Act Three (~25%):
- The Choice (75-85%): acts on what was learned.
- Climax (85-95%): want meets need, protagonist meets antagonist, decisive choice.
- Resolution (95-100%): new normal, confirm the change, release tension, get out.

Once the climax is set, plant its setups backward. The ending should feel surprising and inevitable.
