# Template: Premise-Line

A diagnostic, not marketing. If a blank won't fill, that's a gap in the story.

Formula: When [inciting event] happens, [protagonist] must [goal] or [stakes], but [obstacle].

- Inciting event: the thing that breaks normal life and starts the story.
- Protagonist: who the story happens to.
- Goal: the concrete thing they now try to do.
- Stakes: what is lost if they fail. Make it cost something.
- Obstacle: the force in the way. This is the engine. Weak obstacle, weak book.

Full premise line: [write the whole sentence]

Pressure test:
- The obstacle feels genuinely hard, not a formality.
- The stakes cost the character something real.
- The goal is specific enough to picture.
- You would want to read this book.
- It could not start a year earlier or later without breaking.
