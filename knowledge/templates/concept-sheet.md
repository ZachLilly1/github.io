# Template: Concept Sheet

One page that locks the foundation before any character or structure work. Once filled, it does not change. Copy into a project's concept.md and fill every field. A blank field is a hole in the book.

- Working Title:
- One-Line Premise: When [inciting event] happens, [protagonist] must [goal] or [stakes], but [obstacle].
- Genre:
- The Promise to the Reader: what a reader of this genre expects to feel by the end.
- Protagonist: one or two sentences.
- What They Want: the concrete external goal.
- What They Need: the internal truth they must accept. Usually in tension with the want.
- The Central Obstacle: the force that fills a whole book.
- External Stakes: what they lose in the world if they fail.
- Internal Stakes: what they lose in themselves if they fail.
- Why This Book: the reason it has to be written. Return to this when the middle gets hard.

Pressure test: premise holds, genre and premise agree, want pulls against need, stakes cost something real, clear reason to exist. All yes, or fix before locking.
