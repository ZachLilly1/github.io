# Template: Protagonist Architecture

Build from the inside out. Each layer feeds the next. Copy into the project's characters folder.

- Name:
- The Wound: a past event that taught a false lesson about how to survive.
- The False Belief: the distorted conviction the wound produced.
- The Coping Behavior: how they protect themselves because of the belief. Once useful, now limiting.
- The Want (conscious): the external goal, what they think will fix things.
- The Need (unconscious): the internal truth they must accept. Usually the opposite of the want.
- The Arc: positive (overcome), negative (cling and fall), or flat (hold a truth against a testing world).
- How the Story Forces the Collision: how the plot pushes the want until the false belief cracks.

Test: if want and need don't pull against each other, there's no arc. Push until they conflict.
