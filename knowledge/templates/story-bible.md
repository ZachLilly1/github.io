# Template: Story Bible

The running source of truth for a project, so the book stays consistent across months of work. Lives alongside concept.md, characters, beats.md, and canon.md in the project folder.

- Timeline: day, season, year, and how much time passes across the book. Track character ages.
- Character continuity: names, spellings, physical details, relationships, who knows what and when.
- World rules: how the world works, magic or technology systems, social structures, and their costs. Honor consistently.
- Canon facts: established truths the draft must not contradict. Anything stated on the page becomes canon.
- Setups planted: what's been planted and where, so payoffs get collected.
- Open threads: subplots and questions still unresolved, so none get dropped.
- Continuity flags: contradictions to fix, things to check.

Update the bible whenever the draft establishes something new. When stuck, the problem is usually earlier than where you're stuck; diagnose backward against the bible.
