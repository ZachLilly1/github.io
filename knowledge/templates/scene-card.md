# Template: Scene Card

One per scene. A scene where nothing changes gets cut. Use to plan or diagnose any scene.

- Scene: [name or number]
- POV character:
- Goal: what the POV character wants in this scene.
- Conflict: the obstacle in the way.
- Turn: how the scene ends in a different state than it began. Usually the character is worse off or facing a new complication.
- Enters late / leaves early: where does the scene actually start and stop.
- Connects by: "therefore" or "but" from the previous scene. Never "and then."
- Does double duty: advances plot AND reveals character.

Diagnostic: no goal, no conflict, or no turn means the scene isn't working. Fix or cut.
