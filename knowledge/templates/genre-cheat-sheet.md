Reference:
- Literary: promise depth of character and language, an honest look at a life or question. 70-95k. Watch for plot that stalls with nothing at stake beneath the surface.
- Thriller/Suspense: promise escalating tension and a danger that costs the hero. 80-100k. Avoid the lone-genius detective, the monologuing villain.
- Mystery: promise a fair, solvable puzzle. Crime, investigation, honestly planted clues, satisfying reveal. 70-90k. Avoid the alcoholic detective with a dead partner.
- Fantasy: promise immersion in a world with consistent rules. Worldbuilding through need, magic with costs, rising scope. 90-120k. Avoid the chosen farmboy, info-dump prologues.
- Science Fiction: promise a what-if followed to its human consequence. Coherent idea, consequences that matter. 90-110k. Avoid technology explained in lectures, the illogical evil AI.
- Romance: promise an emotionally satisfying love story with a hopeful ending. Two developed leads, real obstacles, earned connection. 70-90k. Avoid the miscommunication one conversation would fix.

If blending genres, pick the primary for its promises and shelf, borrow from the second.
