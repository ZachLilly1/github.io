# Week 19 — The Devices

## Tools you reach for on purpose

Literary devices are not decoration and not a vocabulary quiz. They're tools that carry meaning under the surface, and by now, with character, structure, and prose under you, you can finally understand what they're for. A device deployed on purpose deepens the book. A device forced in for its own sake reads as pretension. Learn them as instruments you reach for when the moment needs them.

## The core set

Symbol: a concrete thing that carries abstract meaning. Motif: a recurring image, object, or phrase that accumulates meaning across the book. Foreshadowing: planting a hint of what's coming, which belongs with setups and payoffs. Irony: the gap between what's said and meant, or expected and delivered. Imagery: sensory language that makes the reader see, hear, and feel. Metaphor and simile: illuminating one thing by way of another. Juxtaposition: placing two things side by side so each sharpens the other. Repetition: returning to a word, image, or structure so it gains weight.

## How they layer

The power comes from layering. A motif planted early, developed in the middle, and paid off at the climax can carry the theme without a word of explanation. A symbol that shifts meaning as the character changes tracks the arc beneath the plot. The best devices arise organically from the story world and stay slightly ambiguous. Heavy-handed symbolism, a literal storm during the emotional storm, reads as amateurish. Subtle, earned resonance rewards the attentive reader without alienating the rest.

## Thread, don't sprinkle

Don't scatter devices at random. Choose one or two that connect to your theme and thread them through the whole book. A single motif carried from first chapter to last does more than a dozen clever one-off images. Pick what serves the central question, plant it early, and let it grow.

## Task

Choose one or two devices you'll thread through the whole novel, tied to your central question. Write the scene where the first one enters, planted quietly so it can accumulate meaning as the book goes.
