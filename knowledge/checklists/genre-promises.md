# Checklist: Genre Promises

Deliver the convention, subvert the cliché. Check the draft against the promise.

- Have you named the one core promise your genre makes?
- Is that promise kept by the end?
- Are the genre's required conventions present? (mystery: crime and investigation; romance: two people kept apart by real obstacles; fantasy: a consistent system; thriller: escalating danger with real cost.)
- Have you identified your genre's common clichés?
- Have you subverted or avoided each one, delivering the satisfaction through an unexpected route?
- Does the length fit the genre's expectation?
- Does the pace fit the genre's expectation?
- If blended, is the primary genre clear and its contract fully kept, with the second woven through?
