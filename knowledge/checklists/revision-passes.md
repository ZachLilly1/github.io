# Checklist: Revision Passes

Run largest to smallest. Never line-edit a scene you might cut.

Pass 1 — Structural:
- Read the whole draft through, taking notes, no editing.
- Is the protagonist's arc complete and earned?
- Does the plot escalate, or does it plateau?
- Are the stakes clear and rising?
- Does every scene justify its existence?
- Any plot holes, saggy stretches, unmotivated actions, or an unearned ending?

Pass 2 — Scene:
- Does each scene have a POV goal, a conflict, and a turn?
- Does it enter late and leave early?
- Does it advance plot AND reveal character?
- Is it in the right place?
- Cut scenes that repeat or spin. Combine scenes doing the same work.
- Do scenes connect by "therefore" and "but," never "and then"?

Pass 3 — Line:
- Sharpen sentences, vary rhythm.
- Cut filler and filtering.
- Strengthen verbs, remove clichés, fix awkward constructions.
- Tighten dialogue, cut the on-the-nose.
- Hunt your own repeated tics. Read aloud.

Pass 4 — Copyedit (content locked):
- Grammar, punctuation, spelling.
- Consistency: names, timeline, invented terms.
- Fresh eyes: distance or another reader.
