# Checklist: Self-Edit

A fast pass on any scene or chapter before it's called done.

Structure:
- Does the scene have a goal, a conflict, and a turn?
- Does it enter late and leave early?
- Does it connect to the scene before by therefore or but?

Character:
- Is the interiority filtered through the character's wound and want?
- Does what they notice and ignore reveal who they are?

Prose:
- Verbs strong, adverbs cut?
- Filtering stripped so experience lands direct?
- Details selected, not piled? The telling detail kept, the rest cut?
- Free indirect style where the voice should go close?

Dialogue:
- Does it do two jobs at once?
- Is there subtext, or is it on the nose?
- Can you tell who's speaking without tags?

Overall:
- Is the pressure higher at the end of the scene than the start?
- Would you keep reading?
